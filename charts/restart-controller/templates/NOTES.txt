{{ include "restart-controller.fullname" . }} restarts the Deployments, DaemonSets, and StatefulSets that have one of these labels with the value "true":

  {{ .Values.triggers.apiserverRemoval.label }}
  {{ .Values.triggers.crdChange.label }}

Put the label on the workload object, not on the pod template.

Show the log:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deployment/{{ include "restart-controller.fullname" . }}
